## About StoryStick++

StoryStick++ is a novel measurement system that rethinks measurement as an interactive, unit-less process. Inspired by story sticks, century-old craft tools using align-and-mark workflows, StoryStick++ replaces numerical readouts with direct spatial interactions.

The device enables users to measure, mark, and transfer dimensions without converting to numerical values or standard units. Our system integrates a smartphone-based clip-on with embedded sensing and a series of attachments to support a wide variety of measurement tasks for measuring and marking basic and complex geometries with step-by-step guidance.

Together, these contributions offer a new paradigm for measurement; one that emphasizes usability over abstraction and numeric precision.
